# METACITY 3D Asset System — Credits & Licensing

## Architecture Overview
The 3D asset system in `frontend/src/assets/city/` is designed specifically for METACITY. All geometries, procedural shaders, texture atlases, and architectural compositions are procedurally synthesized using Three.js and custom procedural materials.

## License Compliance
- **Procedural Engine**: 100% custom procedural generation utilizing Three.js open-source primitives and custom CanvasTexture atlases.
- **Proprietary Assets**: Absolutely no SimCity, Maxis, EA, or third-party proprietary assets, audio, or textures are utilized.
- **Open-Source Attribution**: Built on top of Three.js (MIT License) and React Three Fiber (MIT License).
